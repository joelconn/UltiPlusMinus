Ulti+/-

Ultimate Frisbee stat tracker for Apple Watch.

Track your stats in real time — right from your wrist. Built for players who want to know their numbers without slowing down the game.

Features

  • Goals, assists, Ds, throwaways, drops & stalls
  • Live score tracking — US vs Them
  • Field +/− and contribution +/−
  • Works for goaltimate, beach ultimate & 7v7
  • Large buttons — works with gloves on
  • Game history on iPhone
  • Crash recovery — never lose a stat

Questions or feedback? Email joelconn@gmail.com

Privacy Policy

Ulti+/- does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. All game data is stored locally on your device. No analytics, no tracking, no account required.
