UltiGoaltiStats

Track ultimate and goaltimate stats on iOS.

Record individual and team performance in real time. Analyze stats across games, tournaments, and seasons. Export to web and GitHub Pages.

Core Features

  • Record goals, assists, blocks, drops, throwaways & stalls
  • 2-point goal tracking with separate shot distance analytics
  • Hockey assists and defensive accountability
  • Live score tracking and point-by-point records
  • Ultimate and Goaltimate modes with mode-specific rules
  • Vic Mode for inter-squad scrimmages
  • Plus/minus and APM (Adjusted Plus-Minus) metrics
  • Career stats across multiple teams and seasons
  • Automatic backup reminders and manual export/import

Advanced Analytics

  • Interactive heat maps showing shot distances and attempts
  • Per-game, per-tournament, and lifetime stats views
  • Team metrics: offensive efficiency, D conversion %, dirty holds
  • Player leaderboards for offense, defense, and contribution
  • Stat editing for manual corrections and overrides

Export & Publishing

  • Export team stats as an interactive HTML webpage
  • Publish stats to GitHub Pages with live updates
  • Link to editable Google Sheets for collaborative analysis
  • Share game stats via CSV or local backups
